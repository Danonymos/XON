# XON Shader 1.7 — Balanced indoor lighting and cleaner contact shadows

V1.7 gives lamp-lit interiors more surface depth and clearer contact shade while
retaining lamp strength, propagation ranges, source glow and XON's warm palette.
The official package is **XON-Shader-v1.7.zip**. This replaces the private
"1.6 fixed lighting" filename; 1.6 remains the preceding AA version.

## Improved indoor lighting

- Stronger face contrast in ordinary block light and colored spill.
- Soft contact shade around solid walls, pillars, beams and architectural corners.
- Smooth skylight blending, with protected emissive cores, foliage and night vision.
- Geometry occlusion reuses the existing local light volume and the AO strength
  control. It requires Colored Block Light; Speed retains its lighter path.

## Corrected partial-block shadows

The previous private revision could treat partial shapes as full-cube blockers
and sample within the receiver's coarse cell. This created false, repeating
shadow patches on flat and inset surfaces.

The corrected implementation starts outside that cell and accepts only verified
full-cube blockers for the extra contact shade. A map of 485 selectors is checked
against installed Minecraft 26.3 models. Partial and unknown shapes retain their
existing colored-light propagation behavior without casting this extra cube shade.
The receiver correction applies across floors, walls and ceilings.

## Retained features

Temporal AA from 1.6, Fast/Off alternatives, PBR/POM support, the existing color
grade, water and atmospheric effects remain included. Lighting propagation and
volume storage are unchanged; additional local occlusion sampling adds GPU work
without another render pass or volume allocation.

## Validation and limits

On the development NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti/OpenGL driver, the lighting source passes
2,088 graphics compile/link configurations with zero shader warnings/failures,
233 lighting comparisons, 29 occlusion checks and 55 shape/capture checks.
The latter reproduce the earlier artifact and cover all six face directions,
partial heights, receiver placement and unchanged colored-light propagation.

These are controlled GPU checks. The user reported improved indoor lighting in
the earlier revision, then exposed the partial-shape artifact in gameplay. The
final correction still needs a live Minecraft visual recheck; full-game FPS,
AMD/Intel hardware and arbitrary resource-pack geometry are not certified.
Individual ray-traced lamp shadows are outside the scope of this update.

See the [verification record](https://github.com/Danonymos/XON/blob/main/VERIFICATION.md)
and [project source](https://github.com/Danonymos/XON).

## Install

Place **XON-Shader-v1.7.zip** in the instance's `shaderpacks` folder, leave it
zipped, select it in Iris and apply. Start with **Balanced + Native** and
Colored Block Light enabled. Revisit the same indoor floors, wall recesses,
ceilings and thin-block edges; compare open daylight and night vision as well.
