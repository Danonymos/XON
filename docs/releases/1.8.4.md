# XON Shader 1.8.4 — Cleaner Lighting & Richer Indoor Shadows

Historical private candidate notes. The public release is [1.9](1.9.md).

This patch builds on 1.8.3's circular lighting and performance improvements.

- Correct bright warm fallback when colored light is blocked, improving lighting
  around quartz pillars, lamp covers and ceiling edges.
- Replace abrupt corner-visibility switches with continuous filtering through
  connected open cells, retaining solid-wall barriers.
- Keep lamp coverage consistent near decorative quartz, reducing abrupt changes
  between colored pools and broad room fill.
- Separate indoor indirect light from dominant lamp identity, preventing
  decorative-source changes from switching broad room lighting on and off.
- Treat opaque decorative emitters as sampling endpoints: their own glow remains,
  while other lights cannot interpolate through solid quartz.
- Reject stale light history when placing blockers or changing source types.
- Remove an unreliable overlap boost that could brighten a light after it
  traveled around an obstacle through multiple paths.
- Retain bright lamp cores and readable rooms within the existing contrast checks.
- Resolve native stair/slab empty halves from block models, allowing light through
  their real openings while retaining solid-side barriers.
- Sample recessed stair/slab faces at their actual surfaces instead of projecting
  them behind neighboring solid blocks.
- Prevent small decorations and planted pots from becoming whole-block blockers.
- Stabilize ambient composition in rooms with partial window skylight.
- Classify open/closed trapdoors by orientation and distinguish their thin panels
  from solid slabs when lighting adjacent surfaces.
- Preserve source colors, radii, circular falloff, smooth filtering, the End
  atmosphere and the previous rendering optimizations.

Test **XON-Shader-v1.8.4-r9.zip**. Shape metadata reuses existing image storage;
conditional sampling adds a small measured cost without a new render pass.
See the [repair validation](https://github.com/Danonymos/XON/blob/main/docs/local-light-repair-validation.md)
for controlled tests, measurements and live-check scope.
