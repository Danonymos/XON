# XON Shader 1.9 — Smoother Lighting & Cleaner Shadows

Indoor lighting now behaves more consistently around detailed builds. Version
1.9 brings together the latest local-light fixes: cleaner illumination on stairs,
slabs and trapdoors, fewer block-shaped dark patches, and a more stable balance
between bright light pools and readable shade.

## Lighting and shadow improvements

- **Better-lit building details.** Native stairs and slabs use their open halves
  for colored-light transport and surface sampling. Open/closed trapdoors are
  classified by orientation, with their actual thin panels used when deciding
  whether a neighboring surface is blocked.
- **Cleaner decoration lighting.** Chains, small decorations, empty flower pots
  and planted pots no longer become whole-block blockers in the local-light volume.
- **More consistent indoor fill.** Covered sea lanterns, quartz bases, pillars
  and ceiling details no longer switch between conflicting ambient-light responses.
  Rooms with partial window skylight use the same stable composition.
- **Smoother transitions.** Surface filtering follows connected open cells,
  reducing abrupt changes at block boundaries while retaining solid barriers.
- **Improved light blocking.** Blocked colored light no longer restores an overly
  bright warm fallback. Newly placed blockers and changed sources reject stale
  light history. Opaque decorative emitters retain their own glow without becoming
  paths for other lights through solid blocks.
- **Familiar XON lighting.** Individual source colors, strength profiles, ranges,
  circular light pools and bright cores remain. Outdoor lighting, the End
  atmosphere, water, materials and camera effects retain their existing behavior.

## Performance and validation

The update keeps the existing optimized lighting pipeline and reuses its current
volumes and compute pass. It removes an unreliable overlap boost and applies
shape-specific work where needed; it does not add a full-resolution light volume
or another rendering pass. Full quartz retains its decorative aura; partial
quartz receives neighboring light without originating a full-cell aura.

The release shaders are byte-for-byte identical to the final 1.8.4-r9 candidate.
Native GPU validation covers 2,310 shader stages and 2,088 linked graphics
configurations, source families, wall barriers, room contrast, shape capture,
5,400 shape-pair apertures and 72 reproduced trapdoor-adjacent receiver failures.
Shader compilation and regression checks pass. These are controlled GPU tests;
the latest trapdoor-room comparison, full-game FPS and broader hardware coverage
have not been independently verified in Minecraft.
