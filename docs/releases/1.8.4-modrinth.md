## 1.8.4 — Cleaner Lighting & Richer Indoor Shadows

Historical private candidate notes. Use the [1.9 player changelog](1.9-modrinth.md) for publication.

- More stable lighting around quartz pillars, covered lamps and ceiling edges.
- Corrected overly bright warm fill when colored light is blocked.
- Consistent indoor ambient lighting when quartz bases cover nearby lamps.
- Opaque decorative blocks keep their glow without passing other lights through.
- Better light-history handling when placing blocks or changing light sources.
- Smooth corner transitions with the existing brightness balance retained.
- Better lighting on recessed stairs and slabs, with their solid sides still blocking light.
- Corrected whole-block lighting gaps caused by small decorations and planted pots.
- More stable room lighting around windows and mixed skylight.
- Better open/closed trapdoor lighting and fewer false dark bands on adjacent blocks.
- Circular falloff, individual light colors/radii and smooth transitions retained.
- Existing performance optimizations retained; shape handling reuses the current light volumes.
