# XON Shader 1.5 — More depth, cleaner POM, balanced light

Bring more detail to your favorite PBR resource packs while keeping XON's warm
lighting, clear water and atmospheric style.

## New in 1.5

- **PBR resource-pack support:** Optional LabPBR 1.3 and OldPBR materials, including
  normal mapping, surface ambient occlusion, height maps and material highlights.
  Tested with Primes HD Textures 256x.
- **Cleaner surface depth:** Parallax Occlusion Mapping adds relief to bark,
  stone joints and other supported textures. Filtered sampling and adaptive
  tracing reduce the striping and broken edges seen on stepped height maps.
- **Natural highlights:** GGX sun/moon glints respect skylight and shadows,
  keeping covered rooms and non-emissive blocks normally shaded.
- **Balanced brightness:** Lower exposure brings brightness closer to 1.3 while
  preserving 1.4's warm palette, grading texture, gradients and atmospheric effects.

Also fixes the Advanced Materials loading failure that could cause Iris to
disable the entire shader pack, plus incorrect emission and missing-normal handling.

## Getting started

Select **XON-Shader-v1.5.zip** in Iris. With a compatible PBR resource pack active,
open **Shader Settings → Materials & PBR → Advanced Materials** and switch it **On**.
Use **LabPBR** for Primes HD. Start with **20% Parallax Depth** and **64 base steps**.

PBR is optional and defaults to Off; Cinematic enables it. Height-map coverage
varies by resource pack, so some blocks remain flat. Higher POM quality costs
more GPU time.

Validated with 1,680 native shader compile/link configurations and 173 focused
material/POM checks. These checks do not guarantee full-game FPS or compatibility
with every resource pack and mod combination.
