## 1.8.1 — A More Atmospheric End

- Lavender lighting gives End islands and structures more depth and character.
- Soft violet-magenta haze drifts through distant terrain, with clear nearby views.
- The galaxy, stars and black hole keep their existing appearance.
- Efficient atmosphere shading adds no extra render pass, texture lookup or buffer.
- All 1.8 lighting, shadow fixes and performance optimizations are retained.

Start with **Balanced + Native**. Tune the effect with **Atmosphere Density**
and **Fog Density** under **Sky & Atmosphere**.
