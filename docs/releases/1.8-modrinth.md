## 1.8 — Better Lighting, Richer Shadows & Improved Performance

A major lighting and shadow update, with more atmospheric interiors and better
rendering efficiency.

- **Individual light profiles:** supported lamps and glowing plants have their
  own colors, brightness and reach.
- **More variety:** softer mint lichen, orange glow berries, yellow torches,
  amber lanterns, blue soul lights, cyan-white sea lanterns and white end rods.
- **Better balance:** bright light pools fade smoothly into readable shade,
  giving walls, corners and rooms more depth.
- **Cleaner surfaces:** corrected carpet-edge dark lines, uneven floor lighting
  and block-shaped light patches on thin and inset blocks.
- **Faster rendering:** optimized lighting and shadow processing while preserving
  the lighting detail, quality settings and PBR/POM appearance.
- **Independent ore lighting:** existing ore colors and controls are retained.

Start with **Balanced + Native** and **Colored Light** enabled.
