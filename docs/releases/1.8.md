# XON Shader 1.8 — Better Lighting, Richer Shadows & Improved Performance

V1.8 brings more character to lighting and more depth to interiors. Supported
lamps and glowing plants now have individual light colors, intensities and
ranges, with bright centers that fade smoothly into readable shade. The update
also improves rendering efficiency while preserving the new lighting and existing
quality settings.

## Lighting with character

- Give supported light sources their own emission profiles, with different colors,
  brightness levels and reach.
- Add softer mint glow lichen, compact orange glow berries, yellow torches,
  amber lanterns, blue soul lights, cyan-white sea lanterns and white end rods.
- Adjust light strength and reach for candle counts, wet sea pickles, charged
  respawn anchors, weathered copper lights and different amethyst sizes.
- Keep ore lighting independent, with separate brightness, color and reach controls.

## More balanced shadows

- Reduce the broad indoor light wash so walls, pillars and corners have clearer
  depth, with smoother transitions between lit areas and shaded spaces.
- Preserve bright light-source cores and enough ambient visibility to keep rooms readable.
- Correct carpet-edge dark bands, uneven floor lighting and rectangular light
  patches on quartz and other thin or inset surfaces.
- Retain the warm outdoor atmosphere and darker outdoor nights.

## Performance improvements

- Share lighting calculations and reuse contact-shadow data to reduce repeated work.
- Improve indoor and outdoor rendering efficiency while retaining lighting detail,
  shadow quality and PBR/POM settings.
- Keep the existing visual presets and quality controls available.

The update passes **2,088 shader compile/link configurations** and **453 GPU
regression checks**. See the [lighting validation](https://github.com/Danonymos/XON/blob/main/docs/lamp-lighting-validation.md)
and [optimization results](https://github.com/Danonymos/XON/blob/main/docs/lighting-optimization.md)
for measurements and testing scope.

Install **XON-Shader-v1.8.zip** and start with **Balanced + Native**, with
**Colored Light** enabled. **Lamp & Held Strength** adjusts lamps and glowing
plants; the **Ore Light** controls remain separate.
