# XON Shader 1.4 — Volumetric atmosphere, wrapping light shafts and richer shadow works

V1.4 overhauls sunlight shafts and atmospheric depth while keeping XON crystal clear,
sharp and fast.

## Volumetric light shafts and silhouette wrapping

A dual-lobe Henyey-Greenstein and Rayleigh scattering model allows sunlight shafts
to wrap, stream and cast dramatic silhouettes around buildings and rooftops when backlit,
even when the sun is occluded. 10 exponential steps with height-stratified air density
cluster samples near structures and occluders, providing silky smooth shafts with near-zero overhead.

An 8-tap edge-preserving bilateral depth filter in composite1 prevents light rays from
bleeding across sharp building silhouettes onto dark facades. Use **Ray Silhouette Wrap**
under **Light Rays** to control the angular spread of wrapping beams.

## Richer shadows and backlit contrast

Shaded building facades facing sunlit open terrain receive warm diffuse secondary ground
bounce and vertical skylight gradients, giving structures full 3D architectural depth
instead of flat gray shade. A subtle backlit grazing rim illuminates silhouette edges
against bright skies.

The new **Ground Bounce** slider under **Lighting** adjusts this response.

## Warmer environment and golden-hour color grading

The environment color grading and lighting have been tuned ~30% warmer. Pale golden,
yellow-amber and subtle warm-red tones enrich direct sunlight, volumetric shafts,
and midtones, while harsh cold blue tints have been removed from ambient fill and shadows.
The 16-cube color grade LUT provides a cohesive, polished golden-hour warmth.

## Atmospheric density and aerial perspective

Atmospheric density settles naturally in lower terrain and courtyards ($e^{-y/H}$),
leaving upper sky, mountain peaks and interior views crystal clear. A forward in-scattering
aura warms the air volume around backlit silhouettes facing the sun, while Rayleigh aerial
perspective gently tints distant terrain with directional sky luminance.

Adjust atmospheric presence with the new **Atmosphere Density** slider under **Sky & Atmosphere**.

## Settings and installation

1. Put **XON-Shader-v1.4.zip** in your instance's `shaderpacks` folder. Leave it zipped.
2. Select it in Iris and click **Apply**. Start with **Balanced + Native**.
3. Open **Sky & Atmosphere** for **Atmosphere Density**, **Lighting** for **Ground Bounce**,
   and **Light Rays** for **Ray Silhouette Wrap**.

## Performance and checks

- Zero additional render passes or frame history allocations; all effects run in existing half-resolution buffers.
- Closed-form exponential and height calculations with branch-free vector math.
- 1215 OpenGL compile/link configurations pass on the NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti driver across all 13 profile variants.
- Pack integrity, option navigation and translation dictionaries validated.
