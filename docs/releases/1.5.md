# XON Shader 1.5 — PBR depth, cleaner surfaces and balanced light

Version 1.5 brings more detail to compatible resource packs: bark grooves, stone
joints and other surfaces gain depth, while smooth materials catch restrained
sunlight and moonlight. The warm colors and atmosphere from 1.4 stay intact,
with lower exposure calibrated against 1.3's brightness.

## What's new

- **Optional PBR materials:** LabPBR 1.3 and OldPBR support, including normal maps,
  material ambient occlusion, height maps, smoothness, reflectance and emission.
  Tested with Primes HD Textures 256x.
- **Cleaner Parallax Occlusion Mapping:** Filtered height and color sampling,
  adaptive tracing and refined intersections reduce bark-ridge striping and
  broken surface detail. Tile bounds and distance/grazing fades keep relief controlled.
- **GGX highlights:** Material-aware sun/moon glints respect skylight and shadows,
  preserving ordinary indoor lighting and cavity shading.
- **Balanced brightness:** Base exposure drops from 0.90 to 0.83 while retaining
  1.4's sunlight palette, grading texture, gradients, ground bounce and atmosphere.

## Fixes

- Correct Advanced Materials compilation failures that could make Iris disable
  the shader pack.
- Keep PBR emission separate from whole-block emission, preventing non-emissive
  specular maps from bypassing the normal lighting response.
- Handle missing/flat normal maps and invalid tangents without disturbing the
  geometric surface normal.

## Enable PBR

Install **XON-Shader-v1.5.zip**, select it in Iris and click **Apply**. With a
compatible PBR resource pack enabled, open **Shader Settings → Materials & PBR**
and turn **Advanced Materials: On**. Choose the material format your pack uses;
Primes HD uses **LabPBR**.

Advanced Materials defaults to Off; Cinematic enables it. Default POM depth is
**20%**, with **64 base steps**. Shallow views adapt up to 512 steps and receive
five intersection refinements. Higher quality adds GPU work. Relief depends on
the resource pack: blocks without usable height data remain flat.

## Validation

- **1,680** native OpenGL compile/link configurations passed with zero shader
  warnings or failures, including PBR-on variants in all three dimensions.
- **137** material/POM pixel checks and **36** quality comparisons passed across
  six Primes materials, three view angles and two texture footprints. Mean
  intersection error fell about **94%** in those controlled comparisons.
- **1,152** diffuse samples were used for exposure calibration. Mean display
  luminance is within **0.1%** of 1.3 in that fixture, with the 1.4 colors/LUT retained.

These are driver and synthetic rendering checks on an RTX 5060 Ti. Final in-game
confirmation of the polished POM and full-game FPS remain separate checks.
