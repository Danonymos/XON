# XON Shader 1.1 — Living emitters and luminous lanterns

- Amethyst blocks cast violet light; glow lichen, ripe glow berries and sculk veins
  gain soft colored auras. Ordinary vines and berry-free stems retain their behavior.
- Quartz already emitted violet light; quartz and smooth-quartz stairs/slabs now
  join the full blocks, preserving their normal texture appearance.
- Regular and soul lanterns have brighter luminous cores and shaded metal frames.
- The existing bloom passes create compact, softer halos with about 30% more
  shared bloom energy, including other bright emissive highlights.
- Occluder-edge interpolation fades tiny sample weights to suppress light flashes.
  The exact reported moving quartz–wood–stone-brick scene needs a live recheck.

## Install

Place **XON-Shader-v1.1.zip** in your instance's `shaderpacks` folder, then select
it in Iris and Apply. Start with Balanced + Native. Colored Emissive Lighting
enables world spill; Glowing Ores, Crystals & Plants controls their surface glow.

## Verification and limits

840 compile/link configurations, 872 GPU cases/comparisons and client mapping
checks pass. The new checks execute capture, compute propagation, sampling,
lantern masks against local client textures and the real bloom shaders.

Existing voxel storage, propagation, fetch counts and passes are retained. Added
material/mask/fade math still has a cost; microbenchmarks do not guarantee game FPS.
Live appearance, the reported flicker and resource-pack variations need checking.
Bloom is a screen-space effect, not a fixed one-block world radius.
