# 1.7 — Balanced indoor lighting and cleaner contact shadows

More depth in lamp-lit interiors, while keeping XON's warm colors and vibrant glow.

- **Better light and shade indoors.** Stronger face contrast and soft contact
  shading give walls, pillars, beams and corners more depth.
- **Cleaner partial-block shading.** Corrected block-shape detection and surface
  sampling address the blotchy, repeating self-shadows seen on carpet, slabs and
  other inset surfaces. The correction covers floors, walls and ceilings.
- **Consistent ordinary and colored lighting.** Nearby colored spill follows the
  same form shading, while lamps retain their bright cores and familiar glow.
- **Preserved lighting reach.** Lamp-strength settings, propagation ranges and
  the warm palette are retained, with smooth indoor/outdoor blending.
- **Retained visual features.** Includes 1.6's Temporal/Fast/Off anti-aliasing and
  the existing PBR materials, parallax, water and atmospheric effects.

Start with **Balanced + Native**. Keep **Colored Block Light** enabled for the
additional geometry-aware contact shading; Speed uses its lighter rendering path.

Development target: **Minecraft Java 26.3, Iris 1.11.7 and Sodium 0.9.2**.
The final shape correction passed GPU regression checks; a live visual recheck
and testing on other GPU vendors remain outstanding. Individual ray-traced
lamp shadows are outside the scope of this update.
