# XON Shader 1.8.2 — Smooth Circular Light Pools & Faster Movement / Warmup

All block-cast light pools now radiate in smooth, soft circular shapes instead of sharp 45-degree Manhattan diamonds, while chunk warmup and camera-motion frame pacing are streamlined with zero loss in visual quality.

- **Smooth circular light pools across all 83 voxelized light families:** 26-neighbor obstacle-checked quasi-Euclidean propagation (`1/8`-block precision), inverse-square cushioned radial falloff, opposing-hemisphere convergence blending, and open-air `3×3×3` radiance smoothing replace sharp diamond edges and square Voronoi seams across all **75 lamp/plant/crystal families** and **8 glowing ore families** (**228 block selectors** total), while preserving deep dark contrast between spaced lamps and zero wall/corner leakage.
- **Faster post-teleport (`/rtp`) chunk warmup & smoother movement FPS:**
  - **Shared-memory voxel compute (`shadowcomp.csh`):** skips inactive `12×12×12` workgroup tiles via a single `xonTileFlags` mask, skips zero/solid neighbors before obstacle checks, and omits stale VRAM history reads immediately on teleport resets (`> 16` blocks).
  - **Moving Temporal AA resolve (`composite2.fsh`):** checks inner `2×2` history depth agreement before fetching the 12 outer bicubic taps and 8 neighborhood variance taps (`+15.76%` faster at 1080p during disocclusion), plus skips 2 redundant center fetches on every pixel.
  - **Near-range POM, fog & motion reprojection (`pbr.glsl`, `atmosphere.glsl`, `geometry.vsh/fsh`, `lighting.glsl`, `composite.fsh`, `final.fsh`, `shadow.vsh`):** hoists atlas texture-size/bound math out of up to 512 POM ray steps and skips sky/Mie evaluation when near-terrain fog weight is zero (`+14.88%` faster at 1080p), replaces `mat4 × mat4` camera-velocity math with branchless `mat3` + right-associated `mat4 × vec4`, precomputes volumetric shaft step weights, and culls non-capturing shadow vertices before matrix transforms when sun shadows are inactive.
- **100% visual fidelity retained:** palette, exposure, PBR/POM intersections, End lavender atmosphere/galaxy/black hole, water, clouds, and AA behavior remain bit-for-bit / `< 2e-6` identical outside circularized block-light pools.
