# XON Shader 1.6-test — Temporal anti-aliasing

A private test build focused on jagged block edges and distant edge stability.
It retains the lighting, palette, exposure and PBR/POM behavior of 1.5.

## Try it

1. Select `XON-Shader-v1.6-test.zip` in Iris and apply it.
2. Start with **Balanced + Native**. Under **Camera & Detail**, choose
   **Anti-Aliasing → Temporal**. No extra mod or NVIDIA hardware is required.
3. For a clean comparison, turn external AA/upscaling off in its own settings.
   Compare Temporal, Fast and Off while looking at the same scene.
4. Inspect diagonal roofs, distant block silhouettes, fences, grass and leaves,
   then walk and turn the camera. Check close textures, moving entities, water,
   POM bark and held items for softness or trails.
5. Compare frame times with the same resolution, scene and effects. Fast is the
   lower-cost alternative and the Speed preset's default.

## Implementation

- Eight centered subpixel jitter positions with checked camera-reprojected history.
- Sharp cubic reconstruction and neighborhood/variance clamping to limit softness,
  color leakage and stale history. Nearest-surface motion at geometry boundaries.
- History resets for camera cuts, FOV changes, invalid frame times, frame gaps and
  size changes; depth validation for newly visible surfaces.
- Unjittered first-person hands and reduced history on animated/translucent surfaces.
- One extra full-resolution pass before motion blur. RGBA16F history uses two
  ping-pong textures, about 32 MiB at 1080p or 56 MiB at 1440p, before overhead.
- Fast uses spatial edge filtering without history; it stays active during movement.
  Off is intended for comparison or another AA solution.

## Validation and limits

The Windows OpenGL driver suite passes 2,088 linked configurations across 87 pairs
and 24 variants with zero warnings or failures, including all dimensions, AA
choices, Boost and PBR. Repeated-frame tests use production GLSL and half-float
history. Lighting and PBR regression checks also pass.

Synthetic edge results improve on average, not at every angle or frame. Camera
reprojection is not a substitute for per-object motion vectors; animated surfaces
therefore use less history. Temporal AA can still soften detail or leave trails.
External temporal AA/upscalers should not be stacked without a separate comparison.

GPU measurements are on the development NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti. AMD/Intel hardware,
live Minecraft appearance, game FPS and compatibility with the user's external
upscaler have not been verified. This test build has not been published or pushed.
