## XON 1.9 — Smoother Lighting & Cleaner Shadows

A lighting polish update for more natural, consistent illumination throughout your builds.

- **Cleaner building details:** improved lighting on stairs, slabs and open/closed trapdoors, with fewer dark squares and sharp bands on nearby blocks.
- **Better decoration lighting:** chains, flower pots and planted pots no longer block an entire block of colored light.
- **More balanced interiors:** smoother light around quartz pillars, covered sea lanterns and ceiling details, with bright light pools and readable shadows.
- **More stable transitions:** fewer sudden brightness or color changes around block edges, windows and newly placed blocks.
- **Improved light blocking:** reduced unwanted light spill through solid walls and corners.
- **Distinctive light sources:** warm lanterns and torches, cool soul lights and sea lanterns, and other emitters keep their individual colors, strengths and ranges.
- **Performance-conscious polish:** existing optimizations are retained, using the current lighting pipeline without adding another rendering pass.

The warm outdoor atmosphere, darker nights and galactic End sky remain part of the XON look.
